Trust records

The trusted baselines and approved change records that pin object checksums across runs. A difference is a planned change only when an approved record pins the exact Test checksum; a checksum that contradicts a trusted baseline shows as unexpected drift. Records are never deleted — clearing or withdrawing keeps them for audit.
