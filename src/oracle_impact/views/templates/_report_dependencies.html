Dependencies

Two views of the same relationships, so their counts differ. "All edges" lists each dictionary or source observation with its provenance, keeping specification and body apart. "Dependencies" is the deployment-impact view: one row per distinct object with its impact classification. Both are read to a limited depth. Linked findings are those whose changed object, affected object, or call path names the edge's object.
